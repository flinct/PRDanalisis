Key Takeaways
1
Focus on High Impact
Prioritize LHL → LHM → MHM.
2
Consider Dependencies
Some high-impact tasks may depend on foundational work.
3
Feature Needs Discovery
New capabilities require PRD/assessment before scheduling.
4
Backlog ≠ Low Priority
Backlog means not ready yet (e.g. waiting for PRD, dependency, or external party).
